The same five steps for every exercise

Open the app with py pc_app/app.py. The top bar should show a green dot and Sensor connected. With no hardware, tick Demo mode.

  1. 1Pick who and what

    Choose the User, the Exercise and Left arm or Right arm. Use + Add user for someone new.

  2. 2Strap the sensor

    Follow the Sensor placement card. The sensor goes on the forearm for curls and on the upper arm for everything else.

  3. 3Calibrate once

    Press Calibrate. Hold the START position still, then the END position. It's saved per user, exercise and arm.

  4. 4Do your sets

    Set the target sets, reps and rest. Press Start Set. Each full rep is counted aloud, and the set ends at the target.

  5. 5Rest, repeat, review

    The rest timer starts on its own (or press Skip Rest). Afterwards, check the History and Progress tabs.

How the app decides a rep counts

The tracker measures the tilt of the sensor between your calibrated START (0%) and END (100%). A rep counts only when it goes all the way up past 85% and back below 15%.

85% Top reached
60% Left the top, now lowering
25% A new rep has started
15% Back at the start, so the rep counts

When something goes wrong

The app pauses counting and tells you why. Nothing half-measured is counted.

Sensor problem: The X wire reads 0.04 VA sensor wire is loose. Check VCC→3V3, GND→GND, X→34, Y→35, Z→32. Counting starts again after 1 second of good readings.
Sensor disconnectedNo data for over 1 second. Check the USB cable and close the Arduino Serial Monitor. The app reconnects by itself, or you can press Reconnect.
Calibration failed: you moved while it was measuringBrace the arm and hold it completely still for the 1-second measurement. Your old calibration is kept.
Calibration failed: the arm only moved 22 degreesThe sensor needs to move at least 30°. Tighten the strap, make sure it's on the right arm segment (see the pictures), and go all the way to the END position.
Can't start: … isn't calibratedEach user, exercise and arm needs its own calibration. Press Calibrate first.
Reps aren't being countedYou may be starting the rep from too high. Go back to the START position until the phase says Ready. If the strap slipped, press Recalibrate.
